CHAPTER 1. INTRODUCTION TO AMQ BROKER ON
OPENSHIFT CONTAINER PLATFORM
Red Hat AMQ Broker 7.14 is a high-performance messaging application that is available as a
containerized image. AMQ Broker supports all OpenShift Container Platform releases that are in the full
or maintenance support phases, as well as releases from version 4.14 onward that offer extended
support.
