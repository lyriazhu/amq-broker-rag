2.1.2. Upgrading a broker instance from 7.0.x to 7.1.0
Learn how to upgrade a 7.0.x broker instance to 7.1.0 for different operating systems.
AMQ Broker 7.1.0 includes configuration files and settings that were not included with previous versions.
Upgrading a broker instance from 7.0.x to 7.1.0 requires adding these new files and settings to your
existing 7.0.x broker instances.
